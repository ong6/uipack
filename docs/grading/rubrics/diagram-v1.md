# Diagram rubric, version 1

Frozen on 2026-11-06. A change to any line is version 2 in a new file; this file never changes.
Score each diagram 1 to 10 per criterion against the descriptions of 3, 6 and 9, citing a
screenshot and a region for every score. A severe flaw caps a criterion at 4. Use it beside a page
rubric when the brief includes a figure, and score the reference figure on it too.

| Score | Means |
| --- | --- |
| 9 | Belongs in a top engineering blog or a design review unchanged |
| 6 | Accurate, but a reader needs the caption to follow it |
| 3 | Misleading, unreadable or decorative |

## Criteria

### G1 One mechanism, shown

Definition: the figure shows how the thing works (the flow, the order, what changes) rather than
listing boxes.

- 3: A row of icons or boxes with no relationships, or arrows that mean different things.
- 6: The flow is drawn, but the key step or the change is not distinguished.
- 9: The reader can retell the mechanism from the figure alone; the key step stands out.

Check: retell the mechanism in two sentences from the figure, without its caption.

### G2 Labels in the right place

Definition: short labels on boxes and arrows, sentences only in the caption; every arrow labelled
when its meaning is not obvious.

- 3: Unlabelled arrows, sentences inside boxes, a legend to decode.
- 6: Mostly labelled; a few long labels or one-item legends.
- 9: Every mark is named where it sits; nothing needs a legend.

Check: count unlabelled arrows and labels over four words.

### G3 Legible at every width

Definition: text stays readable and nothing is clipped at 390 and in dark; a narrow layout or a
readable scroll, never a shrunken drawing.

- 3: Text shrinks below readability at 390, or clips.
- 6: Readable, but the phone view is the wide drawing scaled down.
- 9: An authored narrow layout or an internally scrolling drawing at full size; dark as clear as light.

Check: find the smallest label at 390 and any clipped or overlapping mark.

### G4 Geometry craft

Definition: arrows end at box edges, boxes align to a grid, spacing is even, crossings are avoided.

- 3: Arrows end inside boxes or in space; crossings; uneven gaps.
- 6: Aligned, with one or two awkward routes or cramped corners.
- 9: Every route is clean; equal things are equal; the drawing breathes.

Check: zoom on every arrowhead and junction; list any that miss their edge.

### G5 Consistent with the page

Definition: the figure's type, colour and line weight match the page around it.

- 3: A pasted image in another style, or off-palette colours.
- 6: The page's palette, but a different face or line weight.
- 9: Reads as part of the page: same faces, roles and weights.

Check: compare the figure's text face and colours with the page body next to it.
