# 3D looks

`ObjectScene` (`uipack/objects`) draws one subject (AI, tennis, trading, server, travel, reading,
contact) in one of six looks, the art directions. Studio is `flex`: its night room and rim follow the
site's palette. The other five are `fixed`: their materials and stages are authored and look the
same on every site. A site picks the looks that suit it; each subject offers only the two or three
that suit it. Full reference (API, budgets, Blender sources):
[3D object scenes](../objects.md); the looks model: [Component looks](README.md).

## Picking for a site

- **Pick from the subjects you show.** A spec that shows only a server picks among `realistic` and
  `studio`; listing `paper-theatre` does nothing for it.
- **Studio is the one that follows the brand.** Its night room takes the ink's hue and its rim the
  accent (`studioLook` in `src/objects/studio.ts`, `--tune-object-*` hooks); a `palette` prop on the
  scene overrides both. Pick it when the scene should read as part of the site.
- **The fixed looks bring their own palette.** Paper's viridian and persimmon, Kinetic's brass and
  slate, Cartoon's toy colours, Realistic's real surfaces and Abstract's glass look the same on any
  site. Pick one when the scene is a showpiece, not a brand element.
- **Random or pinned.** `variant="random"` (the default) chooses among the subject's looks per mount.
  A site that picked fewer looks pins one per scene (`variant={4}`) so it never shows a look outside
  its spec. TODO: the player has no "only these looks" prop yet (`src/objects/variants.ts`,
  `chooseObjectVariant`), so a spec's picks are honoured by pinning, not enforced.

## What every look keeps

Labels are crisp HTML in the site's label type, never textured text; mobile paints at 30fps at most;
reduced motion shows an authored still pose; without WebGL the labelled SVG fallback takes the same
region. The player's chrome (Pause, Open canvas, the caption) is the host page's. The shared 3D
style rules are in [Style contract, 3D](../system/style.md#3d) and the shared rendering layer in
[`src/objects/LOOK.md`](../../src/objects/LOOK.md).
