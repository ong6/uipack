# 3D looks

`ObjectScene` (`uipack/objects`) draws one subject (contact, tennis, trading, server, travel; AI and
reading are on their way) in the one 3D look, ink line art. It is `flex`: the drawing stays the same
on every site and takes the site's paper, ink and accent. Full reference (API, budgets, adding a
subject): [3D object scenes](../objects.md); the looks model: [Component looks](README.md).

## Picking it for a site

- **One look, always.** A spec that shows 3D scenes picks `3d: ink`. The owner chose one style to
  stick to and refine (2026-11-15); there is no per-scene or per-site variant.
- **A subject of the site's own is still ink.** A site may pass its own scene builder for a subject
  uipack does not have (`subject`, [a site's own subject](../objects.md#a-sites-own-subject)); it
  follows the same role contract and is drawn in the same look, and it moves into uipack when a
  second site needs it.
- **It follows the brand through the roles.** The faces take the page's paper (`--ui-paper` on a
  page surface, `--ui-surface` on the styled stage), the lines its ink and the one accent part its
  accent, read on the stage at mount and on every theme change. A site whose colours live in its own
  variables points `--object-paper`, `--object-ink` and `--object-accent` at them on `.uipack-object`
  ([how](../objects.md#the-one-style)).
- **Show it on the page.** `surface="page"` lets the page show behind the drawing, which is what the
  look is for: an object floating on the page, not a card.

## What it keeps on every site

One physical object per subject, unlit faces, a 2px ink silhouette and 1px creases at every size,
one accent part; labels are crisp HTML in the site's label type, never textured text; desktops paint
at 60fps at most and phones at 30; reduced motion shows an authored still pose; without WebGL the
labelled SVG fallback takes the same region. The player's chrome (Pause, Open canvas) is the host
page's. The shared 3D style rules are in [Style contract, 3D](../system/style.md#3d) and the
renderer in [`src/objects/LOOK.md`](../../src/objects/LOOK.md).
